# Channel animation format (generated)

Generated by tools/codex/check_channels.py. Private evidence: ui-pose.c, ModelPoseBones (0x80015780).

Enemies, secondary idle actors and stage UI actors use a channel stream, distinct from the player/monkey fixed pose format. A u32 channel count is followed by count four-byte descriptors and then count packed words per frame. Descriptor byte 0 is the one-based bone matrix index; byte 2 is kind 1 rotation, 2 translation or 3 scale. Bytes 1/3 are ignored by this routine and may be nonzero; their broader meaning is unresolved. Descriptors occur in bone order and rotation/translation/scale order within each bone.

All words carry signed 10/12/10-bit components. Rotation multiplies by four PSX angle units. Translation is unscaled. Scale multiplies by 16 in PSX 12-bit fixed point, hence component/256 in glTF. Missing channels use identity rotation/scale and the stored bone offset, including bone 0. Scale multiplies rotation columns before parent accumulation. Frame addressing is header+4+count*4+frame*count*4.

Correction: earlier enemy/idle glTF exports used the fixed player stride. Structural and skin tests alone did not expose that source-decoder error. Those exports and checks are rebuilt using this format and an independent runtime-order decoder. For example UI member 025 has 17 channels (68 bytes/frame), rather than its 16 bones plus two raw words (72 bytes/frame).

Validated 1257 associations / 1257 distinct clip sources / 55009 source frames without decoder disagreement. Observed 5442 scale descriptors across distinct sources. Synthetic checks cover ignored descriptor bytes, parented rotation/translation/nonuniform scale; 5 malformed streams are rejected.

Actual emitted skin positions are checked separately by check_gltf.py and check_preview.py against independent TRS matrices. Runtime actor placement and external animation blends are not part of these base clips.
