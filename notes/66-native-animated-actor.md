## Prepared format and limits

AEACTR1 is a new little-endian asset adapter: 64-byte header (magic, version, node/joint/vertex/batch/texture/clip counts, zero flags and bind-pose bounds); nodes are parent s32 and 10 TRS floats; joints are node u32 and 16 column-major inverse-bind floats; batches are first/count/texture/kind u32; expanded vertices contain 8 xyz/uv/rgb floats plus joint u32; clips have a 64-byte NUL-terminated name, frameCount u32, fps float and frame-major/node-major 10-float TRS samples; textures are width/height/bytes u32 plus RGBA8.

The loader bounds allocations, rejects hierarchy cycles, checks transforms, quaternions, joints, batch coverage, vertices, timing and texture lengths. Current adapter requires the exporter's single rigid-weight skin, uniform samples and TRS nodes. It renders exact stored sampled poses; PSX fixed-point differences and source interpolation limitations remain those documented in notes/50 and 55. Material lighting/blending remains approximate.

This integrates the animated model, not the original PlayerUpdate/state machine, ground/collider contacts, gadget logic, enemies or gameplay. Source-backed ground probing and spawn placement are the next steps. Full results/captures are ignored under `extract/codex/native/actor-validation.json`.
