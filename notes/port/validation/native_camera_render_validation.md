Orbit conversion reconstructs the input eye from the existing renderer angles and target, including its reflected horizontal basis. Invalid projection and degenerate camera inputs preserve outputs; large screen coordinates saturate.

This validates geometry and bounds, not original GTE arithmetic, SDK view matrices, stage camera event dispatch or a console gameplay camera replay. The follow-camera integer core has separate original-instruction tests.
